\section{Conclusion}
MECC couples CPU promotion with retention of the displaced GPU expert in the freed host slot.
A host mirror supports this update without a device-to-host copy.
Placement planning supplies the initial residents; serving requests update their membership.
Across the models and workloads in Figures~\ref{fig:performance} and~\ref{fig:performance_qwen}, MECC improves both TPOT and TTFT relative to the matched Fiddler reference.
The component study separates the effects of initial placement, cross-tier retention, and delivery scheduling.
Capacity and task-transfer results further show how available host slots and online membership affect expert reuse.
