\section{Conclusion}
MECC coordinates offline placement with request-driven HBM/CPU retention and delivery. The evaluated models exceed the available GPU and host memory budgets, leaving HDD reads on the path of first touches and later revisits. A promoted expert leaves a host slot for the displaced GPU expert without a device-to-host copy. Across the A100 and H100 models, MECC improves TPOT and TTFT relative to Fiddler. Component and task-transfer results show the roles of placement, retention, delivery, and online adaptation.
